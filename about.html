About + FAQ

Who I am and the questions people ask.

Hi, I'm Hobie.
I build things.

I work with AI agents every day. They write, build and test; I decide what gets made and check that it really works. That's how I make games, Discord bots, websites and tools, and how I fix other people's projects.

I also do 3D prints. If you need something built or something is broken, send it over. I'll tell you straight if I can do it.

Questions

What can you build for me?

Most things that live on a screen, and things you can hold:

  • Games: a single feature (combat, a shop, a save system) up to a full Roblox game, in Unreal Engine 5 or Roblox.
  • Fixes: crashes, build and packaging errors, engine upgrades, slow games.
  • PC tune-ups: a gaming PC that lags, stutters or crashes, checked and fixed.
  • Discord: a custom bot, or a whole server set up with channels, roles and bots.
  • AI tools: agents that test, record, check or research for you and report back.
  • Websites: a phone-friendly site with your logo, domain and hosting set up.
  • Branding and clips: logos, banners, gameplay clips and trailers.
  • 3D prints: models, figures and custom parts.

If it is not on this list, ask anyway.

Can you work on my existing project?

Yes, most of my work is on projects that already exist. Here is how it goes:

  • Send it over: the project files, a link, or just the error message and a short video of what goes wrong.
  • I look first: I go through what is already there so I understand how it is built before changing anything.
  • I change only what you asked for: your names, layout and style stay the way you had them.
  • You get it back working: with a note on every file or setting I touched, so nothing is a mystery.

Works for Unreal Engine 5 and Roblox games, Discord servers and bots, and existing websites.

Can you do a whole project, not just a fix?

Yes. A full Roblox game, a Discord bot with all the features your server needs, or a complete website with your own domain are all things I have built from nothing.

  • We agree the plan: what it should do, what it should look like, and what comes first.
  • It is built in steps: each step is something you can see or try, not just a promise.
  • You check each step: you see it working and say what to change before the next part starts.
  • It gets finished: tested, set up and handed over ready to use.

This way you are never waiting weeks to find out it is not what you wanted.

What do I get at the end?

Everything you need to use it and keep it:

  • The finished work: project files, a working game build, a running bot or a live website, depending on the job.
  • A plain note: what was made or changed, where it is, and how to use it, written so anyone can follow it.
  • Proof it works: screenshots or a short video of it running, so you can see it before you even open it.
  • It is yours: you own what you paid for.

If something does not work the way we agreed, tell me and I fix it.

Do I pay before you look at it?

No. Message me first with what you need. I look at it and tell you straight:

  • If I can do it, and if not, I say so instead of taking the job.
  • What it costs, before you order anything.
  • How long it will take.

When you are happy with that, you order through Fiverr, so your payment is protected until the work is delivered.

How long does it take?

It depends on the size of the job. As a rough guide:

  • Small fixes (one bug, one error): usually 1-2 days.
  • Discord server setup: usually 1 day.
  • A new feature or a bot: a few days, depending on how much it does.
  • PC tune-ups: usually the same day.
  • Websites: a few days, including your domain and hosting.
  • Full games or big builds: planned in steps, with a time for each step.

You always get the time before you order, and updates while I work.

Which Unreal versions do you work with?

Unreal Engine 5, up to 5.7, in both Blueprint and C++.

  • Older project? I can upgrade it to a newer version first and fix what breaks on the way.
  • Errors after an upgrade? That is one of the most common jobs I get.
  • Packaging problems? I get it building and send back a working build.

For Roblox I work in Roblox Studio and publish the game for you if you want.
