Questions

What can you build for me?

Most things that live on a screen, and soon things you can hold:

  • Games: a single feature (combat, a shop, a save system) up to a full Roblox game, in Unreal Engine 5 or Roblox.
  • Fixes: crashes, build and packaging errors, engine upgrades, slow games.
  • Discord: a custom bot, or a whole server set up with channels, roles and bots.
  • AI tools: agents that test, record, check or research for you and report back.
  • Websites: a phone-friendly site with your logo, domain and hosting set up.
  • Branding and clips: logos, banners, gameplay clips and trailers.
  • 3D prints (from December): models, figures and custom parts.

If it is not on this list, ask anyway.

Can you work on my existing project?

Yes. Send the project or the error and I will look at what is there first, fix or add what you need, and keep the rest the way you had it.

Can you do a whole project, not just a fix?

Yes. Bigger builds are split into small steps, and you see each step working before the next one starts.

What do I get at the end?

The finished files or a working build, and a short plain note on what was made or changed and how to use it.

Do I pay before you look at it?

No. Message me first. I tell you if I can do it and what it costs before you order.

Do you use AI?

Yes. AI agents do much of the building and testing. I plan the work, check every result and only send what works.

How long does it take?

Small fixes take 1-2 days. Bigger builds depend on the size; I tell you before you order.

Which Unreal versions do you work with?

Unreal Engine 5, up to 5.7. Older projects can be upgraded first.
